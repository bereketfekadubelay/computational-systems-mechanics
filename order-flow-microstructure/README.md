 Project 03: Market Microstructure & Order Flow Analysis

 Overview
A quantitative analytical study examining institutional order flow imbalances, volume profile absorption, and delta profile shifts during high-liquidity market sessions (such as Gold and S&P 500 futures opens).

 Methodology & Analytical Framework
- **Core Metrics:** Aggressive delta imbalances, bid/ask volume divergence, and Depth of Market (DOM) liquidity nodes.
- **Behavioral Analysis:** Studied institutional absorption (large limit orders halting market momentum) versus exhaustion patterns at key price levels using footprint charts and order book dynamics.

 Analytical Insights
Microclevel order flow data provides a structural mathematical edge over lagging macroeconomic indicators, mapping institutional execution intent and liquidity resting points directly through tape reading and volume profile analytics.
